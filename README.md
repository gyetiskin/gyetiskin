### Hello, this is Gurur Yetişkin

I'm a cybersecurity engineer, currently Expert Lead at BTCTurk. I began my career in Belgium and France working on offensive security and red team research, then returned to Turkey and built out the rest of it in cloud security, application security and international project delivery.

Along the way: at Deloitte Risk Advisory I delivered penetration testing and security audits to more than 150 banks and 50 multinational enterprises across finance, telecommunications, pharmaceuticals, automotive and manufacturing. At ING Bank I led the RSU team as Lead Consultant, owning pentest delivery, vulnerability management programs and test architecture design. At Paribu I directed Red and Blue Team operations for crypto asset infrastructure serving millions of users. Since 2023 my work has been focused on digital asset and blockchain security.

At BTCTurk I own red team strategy, attack surface management and high impact vulnerability research across the trading, custody and infrastructure layers, working closely with engineering and detection teams. The architectures I design follow OWASP, NIST and MITRE ATT&CK. I hold CISM, OSWE, OSWP, CEH, BHWH, SAST and MCP.

Outside the day job I'm an active member of the OWASP Manchester Chapter. I rank among the all time top 50 researchers on Synack Red Team, YesWeHack and Detectify, and I've placed at international competitions hosted at Black Hat. I like Python, and I have a soft spot for Swift.
